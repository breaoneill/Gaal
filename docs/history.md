# History

## Origin

Seldon began as a personal productivity project.

The original problem was straightforward.

After a night away from work, or returning on Monday morning, hundreds of
emails could be waiting. Most were automated reports, monitoring alerts and
system notifications, while only a small number required immediate action.

Reading every message individually consumed time and increased the risk of
missing important customer communication.

The objective became:

> Tell me what I need to know before I start work.

## Prototype

The first working prototype was developed using OpenClaw running on personal
infrastructure.

Telegram was used as the control interface.

The prototype demonstrated that AI-assisted classification and summarisation
could produce a useful operational briefing without modifying the mailbox.

## Evolution

During discussions with a local business, it became apparent that the
underlying concepts could have applications outside the original use case.

Those discussions led to the separate DigAssiist concept.

Seldon remains the original personal email briefing assistant.
